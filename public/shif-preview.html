Design Concept

Typography

Ultra-strong, thick, and geometric typography crafted to convey structural stability, industrial power, and corporate authority suitable for a heavy-duty freight brand.

Letter 'S' (Infinity)

The letter 'S' is constructed as a dual-colored continuous loop representing the infinity symbol (∞). This symbolizes circular economy, sustainable transport, and continuous motion.

Letter 'I' (Location Pin)

The dot of the 'I' incorporates a high-tech geographic location pin with a hollow core. This signifies precise tracking, cargo destination, and smart logistical routing.
